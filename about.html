Liza Dapper was born in 2003 in Mission Viejo, California. She is completing her BA in Fine Art from Brigham Young University working in printmaking, drawing, painting, and photography. The content of her work is location-based, focusing on the relationship between the land and human impact. She has been awarded the Olena & George K Lewis Fine Arts Scholarship, Experiential Learning Grant from the Department of Art, and was the state winner for the Agriculture and Conservation Photo Contest in Utah. As she spends more time exploring National Parks, her work positions human interactions with eroding geologic landscapes as a symbiotic relationship.
